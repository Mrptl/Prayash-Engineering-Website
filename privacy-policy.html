Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Introduction

Prayash Engineering ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.prayashengineering.com.

Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in various ways:

Personal Data

When you contact us through our website forms, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • Project requirements or inquiries

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited and time spent

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer service
  • Send you information about our products and services
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

GDPR Data Minimization & Sharing

In compliance with GDPR and the Norwegian E-Commerce Act, we practice data minimization. We only collect the minimum information strictly necessary to process your Request for Quote (RFQ) or respond to your inquiry.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We may share information with trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website, provided they agree to keep this information confidential and secure.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights & Right to Erasure

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request complete deletion of your personal data from our systems
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

To exercise your Right to Erasure, please email us directly with the subject line "Data Erasure Request".

Cookie Policy

We use cookies to improve user experience. Our cookies are categorized as follows:

  • Essential Cookies: Always active. Required for basic website functionality (e.g., remembering your cookie consent preference).
  • Analytics Cookies: Require consent. Used via Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website anonymously.
  • Marketing Cookies: Require consent. Used via Google Tag Manager (GTM) for event tracking and service improvement.
