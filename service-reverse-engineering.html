Reverse Engineering

Breathing new life into old machinery. Import substitution and spare part development.

Re-Engineer. Improve. Manufacture.

Industrial machinery is built to last, but specific parts wear out. When OEM spares are obsolete, expensive, or have long lead times, reverse engineering is the solution. Prayash Engineering specializes in analyzing worn-out or broken components and recreating them with original—or better—specifications.

Our process involves detailed dimensional measurement, material chemical analysis (spectroscopy), and creating 2D/3D manufacturing drawings. This allows us to manufacture precision spares for textile, plastic, and heavy engineering industries as direct import substitutes.

Our Process:

  • 1. Analysis: Physical inspection and material testing of the sample part.
  • 2. Digitization: Drafting precise CAD models and engineering drawings.
  • 3. Optimization: Suggesting material upgrades or design tweaks for better longevity.
  • 4. Manufacturing: CNC machining and finishing to exact tolerances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about this service. Can't find your answer? Contact us directly.

What exactly is reverse engineering and when should I use it?

Reverse engineering is the process of recreating a spare part or component by measuring and analyzing an existing sample, without relying on OEM drawings. You should use it when the original OEM is no longer in business, when import lead times are too long, or when OEM spares are too expensive.

What types of components can you reverse engineer?

We have successfully reverse-engineered gearbox internals (shafts, gears, housing), machined castings, custom pins and bushings, hydraulic valve bodies, drive couplings, end carriages, and structural crane components. If you can provide a sample or detailed photographs, we can evaluate feasibility.

How accurate are reverse-engineered parts compared to originals?

Using precision measurement tools (Vernier calipers, micrometers, CMM for complex parts), we achieve dimensional accuracy within ±0.01mm for machined components. Material equivalents are selected based on original material properties. Our parts routinely match or exceed OEM performance.

How long does the reverse engineering and manufacturing process take?

Timeline depends on complexity. Simple machined parts typically take 5–10 working days. Complex cast components requiring pattern-making and casting may take 4–6 weeks. We always provide a firm timeline after an initial feasibility review of the sample or drawings.
