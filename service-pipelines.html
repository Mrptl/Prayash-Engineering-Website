Industrial Pipeline Projects

Expert turnkey execution of utility and process piping networks.

Flowing with Precision

Industrial pipelines are critical infrastructure for power plants, chemical units, and pharmaceutical facilities. Prayash Engineering undertakes turnkey piping projects, ensuring seamless flow and distribution of utilities like water, air, steam, and specialized process fluids.

Our expertise covers both Carbon Steel (CS) utility lines and high-purity Stainless Steel (SS) piping for hygienic applications. We manage the entire lifecycle from layout design and isometric drawing to fabrication, erection, non-destructive testing, and final hydrostatic pressure testing.

Pipeline Services:

  • Utility Piping: Compressed air, cooling water, and chiller lines for manufacturing plants.
  • Pharma & Food Grade: SS 304/316L piping with orbital welding for high sanitary standards.
  • Fire Hydrant Lines: Design and installation of fire safety piping networks.
  • Steam & Condensate: Efficient IBR and non-IBR steam piping execution.
  • Testing & Commissioning: Hydro-testing and radiography to ensure joint integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about this service. Can't find your answer? Contact us directly.

What types of industrial pipeline projects do you undertake?

We handle utility piping (compressed air, steam, water), process piping (chemical, petrochemical), hygienic / sanitary SS piping for pharma and food industries, fire-fighting pipelines, and fuel oil lines. We work in CS, SS, GI, CPVC, and specialized alloys.

Do you comply with IBR (Indian Boiler Regulations) for steam piping?

Yes. Our pipeline teams are experienced in IBR-compliant steam piping projects, including design as per IS 2825, material selection, radiographic (RT) testing, and third-party inspection for IBR registration.

What quality checks are done on completed pipeline projects?

Our standard pipeline quality checks include dimensional verification, visual weld inspection, hydrostatic pressure testing, pneumatic testing where applicable, and radiographic (RT) or ultrasonic (UT) testing for critical welds. Full documentation is provided.

Can you handle complete turnkey pipeline projects including civil support?

Yes. We offer complete pipeline project execution including pipe routing engineering, isometric drawings, material supply, fabrication, erection, support structures, testing, and commissioning. Civil foundation work for support structures can also be arranged.
